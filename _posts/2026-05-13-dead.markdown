#Overview
My project is to bring awareness to the the dead and dying languages in Asia and Pacific Islands, which have been changing due to globalization and shifting cultural trends. The website will contain information on the orgin of the lanuguage, the culture of the communties who speak the languguage, and a basic introductory class on the language.
